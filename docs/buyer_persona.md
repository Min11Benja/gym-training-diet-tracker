# CoachEnControl Buyer Persona - ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

## 🎯 Executive Summary

**Who:** Online/Hybrid Fitness Coaches managing 15-150 clients at $50-200 USD/month per client.

**Core Problem:** Client dropout due to lack of motivation and adherence, not lack of programming quality.

**Current Behavior:** Manual tracking via WhatsApp + Google Sheets, leading to burnout.

---

## 1. WHO THEY ARE

### ✅ YES - Ideal Customer Profile

**Business Model:**
- **Type:** Online coaching or hybrid (online + in-person)
- **Client Base:** 15-150 active clients
- **Pricing:** $50-200 USD/month per client
- **Revenue Range:** $750 - $30,000 USD/month

**Current Tech Stack:**
- Communication: WhatsApp / Telegram
- Tracking: Google Sheets / Notion / Excel
- Programming: Trainerize / MyFitnessPal / Custom PDFs
- Payment: PayPal, Stripe, Mercado Pago (LATAM)

**Geography:**
- Primary: LATAM (Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile)
- Secondary: US (Spanish-speaking coaches)
- Tertiary: Spain

### ❌ NOT Our Customer

- Casual gym trainers charging $20 USD/month
- Fitness influencers selling generic PDFs with no real coaching
- Coaches who don't do actual follow-up/tracking
- Pure in-person trainers (no online component)

## 4. CRITICAL PAIN POINTS

### Primary Problem: **Client Adherence & Dropout**

**Specific Scenarios:**
1. **The Diet Break:** Client eats out/off-plan → Coach doesn't know how to respond → Client spirals → Quits
2. **The Missed Workout:** Client skips training → No accountability → Becomes a pattern → Quits
3. **The Derailed Week:** Life happens → Client falls behind → Coach manually reschedules everything → Chaos

**Impact:**
- **Retention Rate:** Only 40-60% of clients stay beyond 3 months
- **Lost Revenue:** $200-400 USD/month per churned client
- **Mental Load:** Constant anxiety about "checking in on everyone"
- **Time Waste:** 15-20 hours/week on manual admin work

### Secondary Problems:
- **No Centralized Data:** Client info scattered across WhatsApp, Sheets, PDFs
- **Reactive Not Proactive:** Only reaches out when client messages first
- **No Early Warning System:** Realizes client is struggling too late
- **Inconsistent Communication:** Some clients get daily check-ins, others get forgotten

## 7. BUYING BEHAVIOR

### Decision-Making Process
1. **Awareness:** "I'm losing too many clients to adherence issues"
2. **Consideration:** "What tools could help me keep clients accountable?"
3. **Evaluation:** "Does this actually solve my retention problem or just add complexity?"
4. **Trial:** "I need to see this working with 5-10 real clients before committing"
5. **Purchase:** "If this saves me 10 hours/week and retains 2-3 more clients, it pays for itself"

### Decision Criteria (in order of importance)
1. **Does it actually improve client retention?** (Not just "nice to have features")
2. **Is it simple to onboard clients?** (They won't use something complicated)
3. **Will it save me time?** (Automation > More work)
4. **Can I afford it?** ($30-100 USD/month sweet spot)
5. **Does it integrate with my current workflow?** (WhatsApp, Google Sheets, etc.)

### Objections/Barriers
- **"My clients won't use another app"** → Need to show seamless onboarding
- **"I can't afford another subscription"** → Show ROI (1 retained client = 2-4 months of software cost)
- **"I don't have time to learn new software"** → Onboarding must be <15 minutes
- **"What if it doesn't work for my coaching style?"** → Need customization/flexibility

## 9. A DAY IN THEIR LIFE

**6:00 AM** - Wake up, check WhatsApp messages from clients (20-30 unread)

**7:00 AM** - Respond to "I fell off track yesterday" messages, adjust plans

**8:00 AM** - Train own clients (in-person if hybrid model)

**10:00 AM** - Update Google Sheets with client progress, send weekly check-ins

**12:00 PM** - Create workout plans for new clients, send PDFs via WhatsApp

**2:00 PM** - More WhatsApp messages: "Can I substitute chicken for fish?" "I'm traveling next week, what should I do?"

**4:00 PM** - Try to work on business growth (content, marketing) but too mentally drained

**6:00 PM** - Evening check-ins, motivating clients who are struggling

**8:00 PM** - Finally close WhatsApp, but anxiety about clients needing help

**Weekends** - No real "off" time, clients message 24/7

| Tool | What They Like | What They Hate |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| **WhatsApp** | Clients already use it | No structure, overwhelming, no data |
| **Google Sheets** | Familiar, customizable | Manual, time-consuming, prone to errors |
| **Trainerize** | Good for programming | Weak on nutrition, no real accountability features |
| **MyFitnessPal** | Clients know it | Coaches can't see adherence in real-time, no coaching layer |
| **Notion** | Flexible | Too complex for clients, not built for coaching |

**Our Advantage:** Purpose-built for the **retention problem**, not just the programming problem.
